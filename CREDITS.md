# Photography credits

The cultural photographs on this site are placeholder material sourced from Wikimedia Commons.
They are **not** the house's own archive and are intended to be replaced as the family collection
is digitised. While these files are in use, the attribution below must remain available — it is
also published in-product at `/en/credits`.

## Excluded

One candidate image was downloaded during research and **not used**: a photograph of traditional
palace guards in South Korea, which had appeared in a Commons search for Igbo ceremony. It is not
an Igbo subject and does not appear anywhere in the project.

## Brand asset

`public/brand/igweagu-emblem.jpg` is the organisation's supplied emblem. It is reproduced
unmodified — no cropping, recolouring or redrawing — and is not covered by the licences above.
