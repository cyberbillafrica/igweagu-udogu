## Editorial method

The 2021 announcement is written in emphatic capitals and long run-on sentences. It was
rewritten — not copied — into institutional prose:

- sentence case, corrected grammar and punctuation;
- the announcement's three separate thoughts (prophecy, jubilee, registration) separated into
  paragraphs with headings;
- repetition removed; the announcement's `!!!` register dropped;
- no claim added that the document does not make;
- every proper noun, title and date kept exactly: *Nchawaputa ala Igbo*, *Eze Igbo Gburugburu*,
  *Onowu Igbo Gburugburu*, *Obu Gad*, *Eke*, the four signatories and their titles.

## Known textual judgement calls

1. **"The house"** is used throughout as a neutral descriptor alongside the full name. It is not
   asserted as an official title.
2. **"Secretariat"** is used for the correspondence function. The 2021 announcement gives channels
   but no office name; the word describes the function rather than claiming an office structure.
3. **"Directors of Igweagu Affairs"** is the announcement's own phrase and is used unchanged.
4. **"Golden Jubilee"** is the announcement's phrase; "the year the house carries" avoids asserting
   which calendar year the observance closes in.
5. **The four signatories** are described as signatories of a 2021 document, not as current office
   holders.
